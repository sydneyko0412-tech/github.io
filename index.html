Investment Efficiency · Decision Support
Channel ROI & Investment Strategy
Marketing Mix Modeling — 4 OTC & Personal Care Brands
Reframed how 4 brands allocate
marketing investment across channels
MMM 4 brands Channel ROI Budget Simulation
Situation Budget decisions for Tylenol, Nicorette, Rogaine, and Listerine were made without systematic channel attribution — leadership relied on experience and past spend patterns with no way to model the efficiency of reallocation.
Insight Each brand had a different channel sensitivity profile. Tylenol’s ROI was flu-season dependent; Listerine’s was driven by promotion depth at specific channels. A single model couldn’t serve all four — and external variable validation was critical to any credible output.
Action Architected the end-to-end MMM process: aligned 2 modeling agencies and 10+ media agencies on data standards, managed data collection and cleansing, validated external variables (flu index, economic indicators), and translated model outputs into scenario-based simulation sheets for leadership budget planning.
Impact Channel-level ROI insights were embedded into the annual budgeting cycle for all 4 brands. Scenario planning replaced intuition-led allocation — moving marketing investment decisions from historical pattern to modeled efficiency.
Pricing & Promotion Optimization · RGM
Price & Promotion Strategy — Listerine
Revenue Growth Management via NielsenIQ Elasticity Data
Replaced intuition-based pricing
with elasticity-grounded scenarios
RGM Price Elasticity NielsenIQ Demand Simulation
Situation Listerine’s pricing and promotional decisions were applied uniformly across channels — with no visibility into whether the same discount mechanics were generating equivalent demand responses in different retail environments.
Insight Price elasticity differs materially by channel. The same discount depth produces structurally different uplift depending on shopper profile, basket behavior, and platform mechanics — meaning a uniform promo strategy was systematically over-investing in low-sensitivity channels.
Action Built demand simulation tools using NielsenIQ price and promotion elasticity data. Modeled NTS uplift scenarios by channel, identified elasticity gaps, and proposed calibrated promotion options aligned to each channel’s sensitivity curve.
Impact Delivered a channel-differentiated pricing and promotion framework. Provided the commercial team with simulation-backed options to improve P&L contribution and reduce promotional spend in low-return environments.

04 — How I Think

Not a formula.
A way of working through problems.

01
Read the market
Category dynamics, shopper behavior, platform mechanics, competitive signals. Context before conclusion — always.
02
Find the signal
Separate what’s loud from what’s causal. One elasticity curve, one channel pattern, one review insight — the signal is rarely the first thing visible in the data.
03
Translate into action
Insight without alignment is observation. The translation step — from data to campaign, shelf strategy, or investment decision — is where the real work happens.
04
Measure what moved
Not just topline, but mix shift, contribution margin, and behavioral change. Metrics are defined before launch, not reverse-engineered after.
